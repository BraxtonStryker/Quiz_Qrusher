Help

Quiz Qrusher turns your course material into multiple-choice practice quizzes: AI models write the questions as a CSV, and this app runs the quiz, grades it, and shows where to focus next. It works for any subject; the prompts below are the medical-school set, used here as the worked example.

Getting started

  1. Make a question CSV with the AI workflow below. Its header row is Category, Question_Text, Option_1 … Option_6, Correct_Option_Number (the correct option is a number from 1 to 6).
  2. On Home, choose Start new quiz and drop the CSV in. It's kept as a question bank, so you can retake it any time.
  3. Answer the questions. Progress saves automatically; there's no save button to remember.
  4. Submit to see your score, your weak categories and every missed question, then download a follow-up request to target your weak spots.

The AI workflow

Each step uses the model that's best at that job. If you don't pay for one of them, use its free tier until you hit the limit, then switch to whichever model you have.

Turn on each model's strongest reasoning mode (for example Gemini Pro with extended thinking, Copilot "Think Deeper", ChatGPT "Think", Claude's largest model at high effort). Always start a new chat for each step.

Step 1Extract the lecture data

  • Paste slide_miner_v1.md into a new Gemini or Copilot chat and attach your lecture slides (and transcript, if you have one).
  • Save the reply as slide_data.md.

Step 1.5Optional: filter to the objectives

  • Paste data_sanitizer_v2.md into a new Gemini chat and attach slide_data.md, the transcripts and the learning objectives.
  • Save the reply as slide_data(cleaned).md and use it instead of slide_data.md from here on.

Step 2Plan the question style

  • Paste style_researcher_v2.md into a new ChatGPT chat and answer its short questions about your current block (topics, professor hints).
  • Save the reply as question_styling.md.

Step 3Write the questions

  • Paste que_generator_v3.md into a new Claude chat and attach slide_data.md (or the cleaned version) and question_styling.md.
  • Save the CSV it returns, e.g. question_input_10252312.csv (named by month, day, year and hour).

Step 4Take the quiz

  • On Home, choose Start new quiz and drop in the CSV from Step 3.

Step 5Review and follow up

  • After you submit, download the Follow-up request and paste it, with your report and slide_data.md, into Claude for a new set aimed at your misses. Ask it to raise the difficulty if the questions felt easy.

Other subjects: the prompts above are written for medical school, but the workflow is the same anywhere. Use the to make the four prompts for your own course, level and exam.

Taking a quiz

  • Flags: the flag in a question's corner marks it to revisit. You're warned at Submit if any are still flagged, and you can retake just the flagged ones later.
  • Notes: the NOTES tab on the right opens a notepad that's saved with the quiz.
  • Tutor and Test mode: the TUTOR MODE switch above Notes adds a Show answer button to every question. Revealing locks the question. A question revealed before you answered isn't scored ("Revealed — not scored"); if you answered first, it counts as answered, right or wrong.
  • Timer: ⏱ in the quiz bar counts only while the quiz is on screen. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Back: your browser's Back button returns to Home, and the quiz picks up where you left off.

Saving and resuming

  • Autosave: every answer, flag and note saves in this browser right away. The green ✓ in the quiz bar confirms it (hover for details). Find unfinished quizzes under Resume previous quiz.
  • Saves folder (Chrome or Edge on a computer): link a folder in Settings and every quiz is also written there as a .json file. Browsers ask for permission again after a restart, so press Reconnect folder when it appears. Use a dedicated folder; in iCloud Drive or OneDrive, set it to "Always keep on this device".
  • Export and import: Export backup downloads one quiz as a .json file (the questions travel inside it). Settings > Data > Export all saves everything at once. Bring either back with Import previous quiz, which also accepts version 2 .txt saves (with their CSV) and plain CSVs.
  • Browser limits: Safari can delete a site's stored data after about seven days without a visit, and private windows keep nothing. In those browsers, export backups or turn on the save-file prompts in Settings > Saving. The "works best on Chrome or Edge" notice can come back after such a wipe even if you chose "Don't show again".

Install it as an app

On the hosted site, Quiz Qrusher can be installed like an app and then opens without an internet connection. In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon at the right end of the address bar. On an iPhone or iPad, in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (installed apps also keep their saved quizzes longer in Safari). When a new version is out you'll see "A new version of Quiz Qrusher is ready": press Reload and your quiz is saved first.

Review and retake

  • Review previous quiz lists submitted quizzes with their scores. Open one to see its results again.
  • Retake starts a fresh, reshuffled attempt: all questions, or just the missed, flagged, or missed + flagged ones.
  • Category trends appear once a question bank has two or more full attempts, showing each category's score over time.

Follow-up requests

The Follow-up request (.md) on Results is a ready-to-send prompt listing your weak categories, the concepts you missed and the wrong answers you chose. Pick a difficulty and question count (defaults are in Settings), download it, and give it to Claude with your slide_data.md and question_styling.md. You can also download the Quiz Report (.txt) and Missed Questions (.csv).

Lite version

Lite is a single file that works offline, keeps one quiz at a time, and carries its settings inside itself. or use Download for offline use on Home, which bakes your current settings into the copy. Keep it in its own folder (for example "Quiz Qrusher Lite"). In Chrome or Edge, link that folder in Lite's Settings: saves go into a saves folder beside it, and Apply updates the Lite file directly. Otherwise Apply downloads a new copy to put in place of the old one.

Settings

The ⚙ in the top right corner. Appearance (theme, accent color, color-blind-safe colors, reduced motion), text (size, line spacing, an easy-to-read font), quiz options (shuffling, category tags, the submit warning, the Focus cut-off, Tutor mode, timer), follow-up defaults, saving, and data tools (storage use, Export all, Clear all). Changes apply right away.

Privacy

  • Your question banks, quizzes, notes and settings stay in this browser, and in your saves folder if you link one. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The app loads nothing from other sites; its fonts and icons are built in. It doesn't collect usage statistics.
  • The Feedback tab shows a Google Form, which loads only when you open that tab. What you send there goes to the developer through Google Forms, under Google's privacy policy.
