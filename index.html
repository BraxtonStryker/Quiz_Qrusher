Help

The full guide is being rebuilt for version 3. Quick start:

  1. Generate a question CSV with the AI prompts (headers: Category, Question_Text, Option_1 to Option_6, Correct_Option_Number).
  2. On Home, choose Start new quiz and drop in the CSV.
  3. Answer, flag and take notes. Everything saves automatically in this browser.
  4. Submit to see your score, weak categories and missed questions, then download a follow-up request.
